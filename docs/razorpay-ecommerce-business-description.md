# PennyGrade — factual business description for payment-gateway review

**Draft for the merchant to confirm before submitting. This is not Razorpay approval or a category determination.**

PennyGrade offers its own six-month local membership product to consumers. Customers can place an online order for one PennyGrade physical membership card for ₹500, provide their contact and Daltonganj delivery details, and proceed through the membership checkout. Card availability is checked before payment is initialized. The physical card and membership are assigned only after the payment is verified; delivery is currently limited to Daltonganj, Jharkhand. No fixed delivery time is promised on the website.

The membership provides access to PennyGrade's online member account/verification features, participating-business directory, and the current member benefits shown on each partner listing, subject to that listing's eligibility and offer terms. Benefits are provided by independent participating businesses. Each business supplies its own goods or services, decides and applies its own listed benefit where eligible, and collects its bill directly from the customer. PennyGrade's membership checkout collects only PennyGrade's membership price; it does not collect, split, or settle partner-business bills.

The online purchase flow includes a product description, cart, order summary, customer contact and delivery-address form, payment initialization, server-side payment verification, order confirmation, and order tracking. A production payment gateway must only be used after Razorpay has approved the merchant account and the exact product/business description. Do not describe the card as a standalone physical goods sale: its purpose includes six-month membership access and partner privileges.
